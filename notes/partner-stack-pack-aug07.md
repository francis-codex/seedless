# THE THREAD — @seedless_wallet

**1/ no card**
```
Most crypto wallets are built for people who love crypto.

Seedless is built for people whose money is in crypto but whose life runs in fiat.

If that is you, here is the whole thing explained properly. No jargon, I promise.
```

**2/ no card**
```
A wallet looks like one app.

It is actually six separate problems stacked on top of each other, and every one of them has cost somebody their money before.

We did not solve a single one alone. Here is each problem, and who solved it with us.
```

**3/ card `stack-lazorkit-aug01.png`**
```
Problem one. Getting in.

Every wallet hands you twelve random words and tells you that if you ever lose them, your money is gone. Permanently. No bank on earth asks you to do that.

Seedless asks for your face instead. The same way you unlock your phone.

Solved with LazorKit.
```

**4/ no card**
```
Problem two. Paying to make a payment.

On most chains you need a second coin just to move the first one. People sit there holding money they cannot touch because they are missing four cents of something else.

Seedless covers that part for you. You send what you actually have.

Solved with Kora, which sits inside LazorKit.
```

**5/ card `stack-umbra-aug01.png`**
```
Problem three. Everyone can see everything.

A public blockchain means anyone holding your address can scroll your entire financial history. What you earn, who pays you, what you did with it. Your wallet quietly becomes a public salary slip.

Seedless builds a fresh address for every private send, so nothing traces back to the one people know you by.

Solved with Umbra.
```

**6/ card `stack-jupiter-aug01.png`**
```
Problem four. Getting a fair price.

There are dozens of places to swap one token for another and they all quote you something different. Check them all yourself and you lose either money or an afternoon.

Seedless checks them for you and takes the best route it finds.

Solved with Jupiter.
```

**7/ card `stack-alchemy-aug01.png`**
```
Problem five. The boring one nobody ever thanks you for.

Every time you open a wallet, something has to go and ask the blockchain what you own. When that connection is slow or wrong, your balance is wrong, and for about ten seconds you think your money is gone.

Seedless reads and writes through Alchemy. That is why the number is right when you open it.
```

**8/ card `stack-ika-aug01.png`**
```
Problem six. Your money should not care what chain it is on.

Solana is home for us. But nobody actually thinks in chains, they think in money, and money should just move.

Multichain is the next thing we build and Ika is how. It is not shipped. I am naming it here because it is coming, not because it is done.
```

**9/ card `stack-bags-aug01.png`**
```
Who paid for all of this.

Nobody funded us. We won $26,000 at a Bags hackathon in May and built the thing with it. No raise, no permission, no CV, no one's blessing.

$SEED lives on Bags.
```

**10/ no card**
```
There is a seventh problem, and it is the one that actually matters.

Getting money out. Into your own bank, in your own currency, without a service quietly eating one to two percent every single time you do it.

It is not live yet. It is the next thing we ship, and it is the entire reason the six above exist.

September.
```
