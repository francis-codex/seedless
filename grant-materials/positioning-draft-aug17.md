# Seedless Positioning Draft — Aug 17 2026

## Problem
Crypto users in emerging markets earn in stablecoins but live in fiat. Getting money from a wallet to a bank account is slow, expensive, and fragmented across multiple apps.

## Solution
Seedless collapses that into one tap. USDC in your wallet, naira in your bank account, done. Passkey authentication removes the seed phrase liability. The wallet-to-bank flow is the product, not a feature.

## Proof
Private mainnet beta live since May 1. 329 trades, $27k volume through the offramp. 4 Solana hackathon wins including Bags ($26k) and 100xDevs ($2k). 190 tests across 8 security suites.

---

## One-liner options
- Passkey wallet with direct bank offramp for Solana users in emerging markets
- USDC to naira in one tap. No seed phrase. No middleman.
- The wallet that pays your bills.

## Core positioning
Money in crypto, life runs in fiat. Seedless bridges both.
